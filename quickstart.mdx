This guide walks you through creating and publishing your first event on Gatherly.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Click **Sign in** in the top-right corner, then switch to **Create your account**. Enter your full name, email, and a password. Your name is what guests see as the event host.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new event">
    Click the green **Create event** button in the header. You'll see the event form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    Give your event a title and description, and pick a start time (an end time is optional). You can also paste a link to a cover image to make the event page pop.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where it happens">
    Pick **In person** and enter the venue or address, or pick **Online** and paste your meeting link. The meeting link stays hidden — only registered guests can see it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set capacity and visibility">
    Leave capacity empty for unlimited spots, or set a number — when the event fills up, new guests join a waitlist automatically. Choose **Public** to appear on the Explore page, or **Private** so only people with the link can find it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish or save as draft">
    Click **Publish event** to make it live, or **Save as draft** to finish later. Drafts are visible only to you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the link">
    On your event page, click **Copy event link** and share it anywhere — social media, group chats, email. Anyone with the link can register.
  </Step>
</Steps>
