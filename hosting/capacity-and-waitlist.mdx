When you set a capacity on an event, Gatherly handles overflow for you — no spreadsheets, no manual juggling.

## How it works

1. You set a capacity when creating or editing the event (leave it empty for unlimited).
2. Guests register normally until the confirmed count reaches capacity.
3. Once the event is full, the register button changes to **Join waitlist**. New guests are added to the waitlist in the order they sign up.
4. When a confirmed guest cancels, the first person on the waitlist is **automatically promoted** to a confirmed spot.

<Note>
  Promotion is instant and automatic. Since Gatherly doesn't send email notifications yet (coming soon), promoted guests see their new status on the event page and under **My tickets**.
</Note>

## What guests see

- Confirmed guests see **You're going** on the event page and a **Going** badge on their ticket.
- Waitlisted guests see **You're on the waitlist** and a **Waitlisted** badge.
- For online events, the meeting link is revealed only after a guest is confirmed — waitlisted guests can't see it yet.

## What you see as the host

The event page shows a live count — for example, _18 going · 20 spots · 3 waitlisted_. On the [manage screen](/hosting/manage-your-event), the guest list shows each person's status, and the stat cards break down **Registered**, **Waitlisted**, and **Checked in** at a glance.

## Changing capacity later

You can raise or lower capacity any time by editing the event. Raising it doesn't automatically promote waitlisted guests — spots open up as confirmed guests cancel.
