Every event has a visibility setting that controls how people find it. You choose it when creating the event and can change it later by editing the event.

## Public events

Public events are listed on the **Explore** page, where anyone — signed in or not — can browse upcoming events. This is the right choice for meetups, community gatherings, and anything you want new people to discover.

## Private events

Private events never appear on Explore. The only way to reach a private event is through its link, so you decide exactly who gets access by choosing who you share it with. The event page shows a small **Private event** badge so guests know it's invite-only.

To get the link, open the event page and click **Copy event link**, or use **Copy link** on the manage screen.

<Warning>
  A private event link isn't a password — anyone who receives the link (including someone it was forwarded to) can open the page and register. If you need a hard limit on attendance, combine a private link with a [capacity](/hosting/capacity-and-waitlist).
</Warning>

## Which should I pick?

| Situation | Choose |
| --- | --- |
| Community meetup, workshop, open house | **Public** |
| Team offsite, birthday party, invite-only dinner | **Private** |
| Not sure yet | Start **Private**, switch to Public later by editing the event |
