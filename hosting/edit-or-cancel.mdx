Plans change. Here's how edits and cancellations work, and what your guests experience.

## Editing an event

Open the event under **My events** and click **Edit**. You can change every detail — title, description, time, location, meeting link, capacity, and visibility — whether the event is a draft or already published.

A few things to know:

- Changes are live immediately. Guests who open the event page see the updated details.
- Guests are **not notified** of changes (email notifications are coming soon), so for important updates like a new time or venue, reach out to guests directly.
- Switching a published event from Public to Private removes it from Explore, but existing registrations stay intact.
- Changing the meeting link of an online event updates what confirmed guests see on the event page.

## Cancelling an event

At the bottom of the manage screen, click **Cancel event** and confirm. Cancelling:

- Marks the event with a red **Cancelled** badge on its page and on every guest's ticket.
- Closes registration — nobody new can register or join the waitlist.
- Keeps the page visible, so guests who visit it see that it's off.

<Warning>
  Cancelling can't be undone from the app, and guests aren't emailed about it — let them know directly, especially close to the event date.
</Warning>

## Deleting vs. cancelling

Gatherly keeps cancelled events around rather than deleting them, so guests always have an answer when they check their ticket. Past events move to the **Past** section of My events automatically once their start time passes.
