Everything you host lives under **My events** in the top menu, sorted into Upcoming, Drafts, and Past. Click any event to open its manage screen.

## At a glance

Three stat cards summarize your event in real time:

- **Registered** — confirmed guests with a spot.
- **Waitlisted** — guests waiting for a spot to open.
- **Checked in** — guests you've marked as arrived.

## The guest list

Below the stats, every guest is listed with their name and status. From here you can:

- **Check in** guests as they arrive — tap the button next to a name to toggle it. Perfect for working the door from your phone.
- **Copy guest list** — copies the full list (names, statuses, and check-in marks) to your clipboard, ready to paste into a note or spreadsheet.

## Quick actions

At the top of the manage screen:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **View page** | Opens the public event page, exactly as guests see it. |
| **Edit** | Opens the event form to change any detail. |
| **Copy link** | Copies the shareable event link. |

## Sharing your event

The event link is the heart of Gatherly — share it in group chats, on social media, or anywhere your audience is. Public events also get discovered on Explore; private events rely on the link entirely.
