Gatherly is a simple, delightful way to bring people together. Hosts create in-person or online events and share them with the world. Guests discover events, register in one click, and keep every ticket in one place. All events on Gatherly are free.

## One account, two hats

Every Gatherly account can both host and attend. There is no separate "organizer" signup — create an account once, then:

- **Host** — use **Create event** to publish events, and **My events** to manage them.
- **Attend** — browse **Explore**, register for events, and find everything under **My tickets**.

## Free and simple, by design

Gatherly currently focuses on free events, so there is nothing to configure around pricing or payouts. Paid tickets and email notifications are **coming soon** — see the [FAQ](/help/faq) for what's on the roadmap.
