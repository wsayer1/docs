Attending an event on Gatherly takes about a minute. Here's the whole journey.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find an event">
    The **Explore** page lists every upcoming public event. Browse it freely — no account needed. If someone shared a private event link with you, just open the link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in or create an account">
    Registering requires an account. If you're not signed in, clicking **Sign in to register** takes you to the sign-in page, where you can also create an account with your name, email, and a password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register">
    On the event page, click **Register**. If the event is full, you'll see **Join waitlist** instead — you'll be promoted automatically when a spot opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You're in">
    The event page now shows **You're going** (or **You're on the waitlist**). For online events, the meeting link appears on the page as soon as your spot is confirmed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Where to find your ticket

Everything you've registered for lives under **My tickets** in the top menu, sorted into upcoming and past events. Each ticket shows whether you're **Going** or **Waitlisted**. See [My tickets](/attending/my-tickets) for details.

<Note>
  Gatherly doesn't send email confirmations yet (coming soon) — your ticket in **My tickets** is your confirmation.
</Note>
