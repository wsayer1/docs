## Events and tickets

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do events cost anything?">
    No. Every event on Gatherly is free — for hosts to create and for guests to attend. Paid tickets are **coming soon**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will I get an email confirmation when I register?">
    Not yet — email confirmations and reminders are **coming soon**. Your ticket under **My tickets** is your confirmation, and the event page always shows your current status.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I know if I got off the waitlist?">
    Check the event page or **My tickets**: your badge switches from **Waitlisted** to **Going** the moment a spot opens for you. Promotion is automatic and first come, first served.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I transfer my ticket to someone else?">
    Not directly. Tickets are tied to your account. You can cancel your registration to free up the spot — but note the spot goes to the first person on the waitlist, not necessarily your friend.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a QR code for check-in?">
    No — hosts check guests in by name from their guest list. Just give your name at the door.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Hosting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I un-cancel an event?">
    No. Once cancelled, an event stays cancelled. If plans are back on, create a fresh event and share the new link.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I turn a published event back into a draft?">
    No — publishing is one-way. If you need to hide a published event, switch its visibility to **Private** so it leaves Explore, or cancel it entirely.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will guests be notified if I change the time or venue?">
    Not automatically — notifications are **coming soon**. Guests see the new details when they open the event page, but for important changes, reach out to them directly.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Who can see my online event's meeting link?">
    Only you and guests with a confirmed spot. Waitlisted guests and casual visitors can't see it, even on a public event.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Coming soon

A few things on the Gatherly roadmap:

- **Paid tickets** — sell tickets and get paid for your events.
- **Email notifications** — confirmations, reminders, waitlist promotions, and change alerts.

Until then, Gatherly stays intentionally simple: free events, instant registration, and everything you need on the event page.
