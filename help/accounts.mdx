## Creating an account

Click **Sign in** in the top-right corner, then switch to **Create your account**. You'll need:

- **Full name** — shown publicly when you host ("Hosted by …") and visible to hosts on their guest lists when you register.
- **Email and password** — your sign-in credentials. There's no email confirmation step; your account works immediately.

## One account for everything

There's no separate host or guest account type. Every Gatherly account can:

- Create, publish, and manage events (**Create event**, **My events**)
- Register for other people's events (**Explore**, **My tickets**)

You can even register for events while hosting your own — the two sides never interfere.

## What needs an account (and what doesn't)

| Action | Account required? |
| --- | --- |
| Browse Explore and view public event pages | No |
| Open a private event link | No |
| Register for an event | **Yes** |
| See an online event's meeting link | **Yes** (and a confirmed spot) |
| Create and manage events | **Yes** |

## Signing out

Click **Sign out** in the top-right corner. Your events, registrations, and tickets are all saved — they'll be right where you left them when you sign back in.

## Trouble signing in?

- _"Incorrect email or password"_ — double-check both; passwords are case-sensitive.
- _"An account with this email already exists"_ — you've signed up before; switch to **Sign in** instead.
