**My tickets** in the top menu is your personal event hub — every registration you've made, kept tidy automatically.

## How it's organized

- **Upcoming** — events that haven't happened yet, sorted by date so the next one is on top.
- **Past & cancelled** — events that already took place, plus any that were cancelled by the host.

Click any ticket to open the full event page.

## Reading your ticket

Each ticket carries a status badge:

| Badge | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Going** | You have a confirmed spot. For online events, the meeting link is on the event page. |
| **Waitlisted** | You're in the queue. You'll be promoted automatically when a spot opens. |
| **Cancelled** | The host cancelled the event. |

<Note>
  Your ticket is your confirmation — there's no email or QR code (email confirmations are coming soon). At in-person events, the host checks you in by name from their guest list.
</Note>

## Removing a ticket

To remove an upcoming event from your tickets, open it and click **Cancel registration**. Past and cancelled events stay in your history.
