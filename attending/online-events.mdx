Online events on Gatherly work just like in-person ones, with one twist: the meeting link is protected.

## Finding the link

The meeting link lives on the event page, right under the **Online event** label. It appears there **only when your registration is confirmed**:

| Your status | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| Not registered | "The meeting link is revealed after you register." |
| Waitlisted | The link stays hidden until you're promoted to a confirmed spot. |
| **Going** (confirmed) | The full meeting link, ready to click. |

This keeps the link out of the hands of people who never registered, even if the event page itself is public.

## Before the event

1. Open **My tickets** and click the event.
2. Confirm your badge says **Going** — if it says **Waitlisted**, you don't have a spot yet.
3. Click the meeting link on the event page at start time. It opens in a new tab.

<Tip>
  Hosts can update the meeting link at any time, so grab it from the event page shortly before the event rather than saving it days in advance.
</Tip>

## For hosts

As the host, you always see your own event's meeting link on the event page, and you can change it via **Edit** on the manage screen. The link is required when you pick **Online** as the location.
